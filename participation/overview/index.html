Token and participation

Stake JURIS to participate in reward distributions. Your position type and tier determine its reward weight and release conditions. Available rewards depend on the assets funded into the staking contract.

Find JURIS

Section titled “Find JURIS”

Use Token addresses to identify JURIS on each network, Markets & listings for trading pairs and Bridge JURIS for cross-chain routes.

Choose a staking position

Section titled “Choose a staking position”

Juris supports Standard, Bronze, Silver and Gold positions. Before staking, review the tier’s current multiplier and release conditions. Higher weight increases a position’s relative share of eligible rewards; it does not set a fixed APR.

Use the staking guide to stake, upgrade a tier or withdraw released tokens.

Claim rewards

Section titled “Claim rewards”

The staking page displays your claimable reward assets. Select the claim action and confirm the transaction in your wallet. Historical APR measures recent funding relative to staked value; it is not a forecast.

Read Rewards for the calculation and funding sources.

Gold promotion

Section titled “Gold promotion”

The Bumeo Shirini Promotion allocates Bumeo’s ordinary share of eligible DEX revenue to additional distributions for eligible Gold stakers during the promotion period. Gold eligibility is determined at the relevant snapshot.

Governance

Section titled “Governance”

Juris includes proposal and voting contracts. The September 22, 2026 mainnet staking configuration had no governance contract linked. Staking therefore does not establish control over money-market parameters in that configuration. See Governance and control.

Staking JURIS is separate from lending assets, depositing borrowing collateral or delegating LUNC to a Terra Classic validator. Learn more about the JURIS token.
